Why MARRA

A name that means connection

MARRA is inspired by Aboriginal language — meaning connection, helping hands, and working together. Those three ideas are our blueprint: a place where neighbours become a community, where no one is left to manage alone, and where everyone has something to give.

We don't run programs for the community. We build them with it.

What we do

Programs built around people

Six areas of support, each shaped by what the community tells us it needs.

Family Support

Practical, judgement-free help for families navigating everyday pressures.

  • Parenting circles & peer support
  • Material aid referrals
  • School-holiday family days

Educational

Lifelong learning — from literacy and digital skills to language exchange.

  • Adult literacy & English
  • Digital confidence sessions
  • Homework & study help

Wellbeing & Health

Gentle, accessible activities for body and mind, close to home.

  • Movement & gentle exercise
  • Mental-health check-ins
  • Healthy cooking groups

Youth Development

Spaces where young people build skills, confidence and belonging.

  • Mentoring & leadership
  • Creative & sports clubs
  • Pathways to work

Elder Care

Connection and dignity for older neighbours, countering isolation.

  • Social & cultural mornings
  • Friendly check-in calls
  • Tech & transport help

Inclusion & Advocacy

Making sure every voice is heard, and every door is open.

  • Accessibility-first design
  • Multicultural programming
  • Community advocacy

Transparent by design

We're a not-for-profit, and we act like one. Good governance isn't paperwork to us — it's how we keep faith with the community and the people who support us.

Registered not-for-profit structure
Governed by a volunteer committee under a public constitution.
Open financial reporting
Income and spending published in plain language each year.
Child-safe & volunteer screening
Working-with-children checks and a clear safeguarding policy.
Community-led decisions
Programs reviewed with the people who use them, not just for them.
